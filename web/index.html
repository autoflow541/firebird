What it is

One console for the whole act

Firebird runs your visuals, projection mapping, lasers and stream from one place — and it keeps running if the internet drops. It doesn't replace Resolume or Pangolin; it conducts your show and safely ties your tools together, synced to Ableton, with a phone remote for when your hands are full.

Features

Built for a live room

Everything below is on one screen, always a click away — with the controls that matter most kept front and centre.

Clock

Ableton-synced

Ableton Live is the show clock over OSC and Ableton Link. No competing timeline.

Safety

Master blackout

A latching blackout that overrides scenes, timelines, effects and remote commands — instantly.

Visuals

32 Blaize presets over TCP, plus a Kinect depth-FX layer with motion tracers and hypercolor.

Mapping

Projection mapping

Photo-from-the-projector workflow: draw surfaces, corner-pin your visuals onto real walls and set pieces.

Laser

Safety-gated laser

ILDA / ShowNET / DMX output behind a hardware-interlock model. Blackout always kills the beam.

Stream

OBS integration

A browser-source background/overlay for OBS, plus start/stop/scene control from the console or a cue.

Reactive

Sound-reactive

The room's audio drives your visuals — and an armed laser — live, with tunable sensitivity.

Remote

Phone remote

Open a token-protected link on any phone on the Wi-Fi. Big scenes, transport, and a blackout you can always hit.

Control

MIDI & AR

MIDI-learn any pad or knob (Push 2 ready), plus a webcam AR filter for a bit of fun on the screen.

A note on safety

Firebird is built safety-first: master blackout latches and overrides everything, and laser output stays disabled until a hardware interlock and key are in place — software is never the only thing between "off" and "firing." Lasers are powerful; a Class 3B/4 laser needs a physical interlock, eye protection, and (for audience scanning in the US) an FDA/CDRH variance and a laser-safety officer. Use responsibly.
