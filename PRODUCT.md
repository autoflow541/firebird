# Firebird — Product Strategy

## Positioning
> **Firebird is the free, local-first show conductor.** Ableton is your clock, your
> phone is your remote, master blackout is sacred. It runs your visuals, projection
> mapping, lasers, and stream from one place — and syncs/orchestrates the tools you
> already use — for a solo performer, small band, DJ, or live streamer.

The trap to avoid: trying to *out-feature* Resolume / MadMapper / Pangolin /
grandMA individually. We don't win that. We win by being the **free, Ableton-
clocked, phone-remote, safety-first conductor** that ties an act together and
**interops** with the deep specialists.

## Competitive landscape
| Job | Leaders | Firebird's stance |
|---|---|---|
| Projection mapping | HeavyM, MadMapper, Resolume | Basic corner-pin built in; interop for advanced |
| VJ / visuals | Resolume, VDMX, TouchDesigner | Sources + audio-reactive; take NDI/Spout in |
| DMX lighting | QLC+, Lightkey, LightJams | Master + laser; send Art-Net to QLC+ |
| Laser | Pangolin, Laserworld Showcode | **We are the safety + blackout + trigger master** |
| Streaming | OBS | Feed + control OBS (done) |
| Audio-reactive | Resolume, Synesthesia, LightJams | Core strength — everything reacts |
| Show glue / sync | Companion, Chataigne, Ableton Link | **Our wedge — go deep** |

## Differentiators we own (keep investing)
- **Ableton as clock** (OSC + now **Ableton Link**) · **phone remote** · **latching
  master blackout** · **laser safety model** (interlock/arm/blackout) · **audio-
  reactive across all outputs** · **WCAG 2.2 AA** (no competitor does this) ·
  **free & local-first** (runs with no internet).
- Kept by owner's direction: **laser** (real DS-1000RGB in use), **Kinect v2 depth
  FX**, **map-a-human AR**, **AR sombrero filter**.

## Roadmap
### Done
- Hardened safety core · laser (ILDA/ShowNET/DMX + sound-reactive-when-armed) ·
  Kinect depth FX · webcam · projection mapping · map-a-human AR · **AR sombrero
  filter** · streaming/OBS · sound-reactive · one-screen tabbed UX · WCAG 2.2 AA ·
  FOH keyboard shortcuts · show files · **MIDI-learn** · **Ableton Link** (optional).

### Next (highest leverage — interop so we play in the pro stack)
- **NDI in/out** — receive Resolume/TouchDesigner as a source; send Firebird's
  output to OBS. (Needs a native module — `grandiose`/NDI SDK; build step.)
- **Spout/Syphon** — GPU texture sharing with MadMapper/Resolume. (Native.)
- **Media/clip playback** — image/video sources (VJ table stakes).
- **Visual cue-list editor** — edit cues in-UI (today: show-file JSON).

### Later
- Fixture profiles/library (or full QLC+ Art-Net hand-off) · timecode LTC/MTC for
  festival slots · blend modes/layers on the visual output · mesh-warp mapping.

## Business
Free (Auto-Flow suite lead-gen + portfolio + community). Competitors are paid
(Resolume/MadMapper/Pangolin) or free-but-siloed (QLC+/OBS). Free + all-in-one +
Ableton-native + accessible is a genuine adoption wedge. Keep it free.
